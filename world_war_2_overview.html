World War 2 Overview

This page is about the global conflict that took place from 1939 to 1945.

The Sides | Key Dates | Aftermath | Resources

The two sides

The war was fought between two major groups: the Allies and the Axis powers.

1. The Allies

  • United States
  • United Kingdom
  • France
  • Soviet Union
  • China

2. The Axis

  • Germany
  • Italy
  • Japan

Key dates

  • 1939: Germany invades Poland, starting the war
  • 1941: Japan attacks Pearl Harbor, bringing the US into the war
  • 1944: D-Day, the Allied invasion of Normandy
  • 1945: Germany surrenders in May; Japan surrenders in September, ending the war

"History is written by the victors, but the cost of global conflict is paid by everyone."

Aftermath and Legacy

Following the conclusion of World War 2 in 1945, the global landscape changed dramatically. The United Nations was established to promote international cooperation and prevent future conflicts. The war also led to the decolonization of many regions and the emergence of the United States and the Soviet Union as superpowers.
